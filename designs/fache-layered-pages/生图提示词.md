# 下班发车 · 插画生图提示词包 v1

> 用法：每条 prompt = 【风格基底】+【单图描述】直接拼接后发给生图模型。
> 统一要求：透明底 PNG（除非注明），单主体居中，不要任何文字/字母出现在画面里。
> 生成后替换设计板 defs 里同名 SVG symbol（落位见各条）。

## 风格基底（每条前置拼接）

```
Scandinavian flat illustration, paper cut-out style, matte solid colors, no outlines,
soft rounded geometric shapes, minimal facial features, thin delicate whisker/detail lines,
children's picture book aesthetic, generous negative space, subtle soft drop shadow only,
no gradients, no text, no letters. Transparent background PNG.
```

```
one caramel #C6884A patch over the right ear with a small dark brown #78441F spot,
tiny triangle ears, sleepy closed eyes drawn as two thin curved lines, tiny pink triangle nose,
three thin whiskers on each side extending beyond the face, caramel tail curling up beside the body,
front view, centered.
```
